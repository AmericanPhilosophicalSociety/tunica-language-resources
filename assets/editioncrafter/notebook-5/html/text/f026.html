Record Number 43

Bear Dance

August 4, 1933

This is a good record.

Singing and

drumming are both recorded.

The first two

renditions are with drum and singing both

in mouthpiece.

The last three renditions are

with singing close in mouthpiece.

The

pitch A is recorded at the end of the record.

The machine was running on Slow.

The song is supposed to be sung

four times.
