# {{ page.title }}

<p>In addition to the original notebooks recorded by Mary Haas and Sesostrie Youchigant, there are many other types of unique archival documents about the Tunica people and language.</p>

{% include view-all-resources.html %}

<section class="textWithImage">
<div class="manuscripts1">

  <p>The resources include a wide variety of materials:</p>

  <ul>
    <li>Letters, including some from Tunica people such as Sesostrie Youchigant and Chief Joe Pierite</li>
    <li>More original notes about the Tunica language, such as vocabularies about <a href="https://diglib.amphilsoc.org/islandora/object/text%3A349645" target="_blank">plants and animals</a></li>
    <li>Notes by Haas and other researchers on a wide range of cultural and historical topics such as <a href="https://diglib.amphilsoc.org/islandora/object/text%3A349830" target="_blank">the Tunica and other neighboring Indigenous peoples</a></li>
  </ul>

</div>
