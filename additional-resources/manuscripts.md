# Additional Manuscripts

In addition to the original notebooks recorded by Mary Haas & Sesostrie Youchigant, there are many other types of unique archival documents about the Tunica people and language. 

View a listing of [all Tunica manuscript resources](https://diglib.amphilsoc.org/solr-search/content?search_api_fulltext=tunica&sort_by=search_api_relevance&sort_order=DESC&items_per_page=10&f%5B0%5D=genre%3AManuscripts).

The resources include a wide variety of materials:

+ letters, including some from Tunica people such as Sesostrie Youchigant and Chief Joe Pierite
+ more original notes about the Tunica language, such as vocabularies about [plants and animals](https://diglib.amphilsoc.org/islandora/object/text%3A349645)
+ Notes by Haas and other researchers on a wide range of cultural and historical topics such as [the Tunica and other neighboring Indigenous peoples](https://diglib.amphilsoc.org/islandora/object/text%3A349830)
