Records taken in 1937

Record Number 63

Part of the Sun-Dance song - as

much as Sesostrie could remember.

Words

ha-liⁿ ču-ku-wi

ča-ni ča-ni ha-li-ču-ku-wi

ča-ni ča-ni [ ča-ni ]*

ha-li-ču-ku-wi ča-ni :||

* Sometimes three ča-nis and sometimes two

Sesostrie thinks the words are Tunica, and

tries to make it hali n čuʔonta v.I -

they took the land.

It may be Tunica, but

is not intelligible today.
